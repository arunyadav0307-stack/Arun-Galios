## ⚠ CAVEAT — read this before quoting anything

**PDF text extraction mangles much of the mathematics.** Superscripts are lost,
symbols are garbled, and multi-line displays are flattened. This is already
known to be the origin of at least one recorded anomaly (see `BLUEPRINT.md`
Annex A.5, flag **F2**, whose caveat explicitly says "the PDF text extraction
mangled much of the mathematics … so a transcription error on our side cannot
be excluded"; the same suspicion attaches to flag **F3**).

Therefore:

- These files are authoritative for **locating** a statement, definition or
  table by page number.
- They are **NOT** authoritative for the **content** of a formula. Any formula
  used in the paper must be re-read from the rendered PDF before it is trusted.
- Cross-checks **XC-12** (flag F3) and **XC-16** (flag F2) in
  `EXECUTION_PLAN.md` §D require re-reading the *original PDF*, for exactly this
  reason.
